How to use the app

Written from the app as it is now, one part at a time, each with its screen. Jump around from the contents.

Three minutes a day where you go nowhere?

Fudo is an app for practicing this every day: a few minutes sitting still, and moments while walking, washing dishes or commuting, all practice in knowing you're here.

First launch

  1. Six short pages on why it works this way come first; Skip is at the top right.
  2. Hold the flame; when it lights up, you've started: 5 seconds first, then one more minute, or later.
  3. Choose how long you'll usually sit, then whether you want a notice when time's up.
  4. Back on home, a short guide has you swipe each of the four directions once.

Hold the flame to begin

Home: one tower, four directions

In the middle is your own tower. Swipe from the middle in any of four directions to go somewhere.

  1. Tap the flame or a stone and it reacts; hold the flame to start sitting.
  2. Tap an empty spot: Outfits appears top left and Settings top right, then they fade after a few seconds.
  3. The line at the bottom shows your total still minutes. Every 300 minutes adds a stone you can stack on your tower.

Still: sit and know you're here

  1. Hold the flame. The glow grows; when it lights up, you've started.
  2. The screen dims. When you notice you've drifted, tap anywhere once to count it.
  3. When time's up your phone buzzes twice; if you don't stop, it keeps counting. Hold the flame again to stop.
  4. You get a result like “5 min · Drifts: 3”. Add a line if you like, then tap Put in the tower.

Moving: in everyday life

  1. Swipe right from home, pick what you're doing and for how long, and tap Start.
  2. While you do it, notice whether you're here; when you drift, tap Drifted.
  3. You can close the app: the lock screen and Dynamic Island show the time, and you can mark drifts or end there.
  4. Back in the app, tap Keep to count it today, or Don't count to leave no record.

Closing the night

  1. After sunset, on a day you practiced, home turns to night and a close-the-day button appears at the bottom.
  2. Answer one question: today, were there moments you remembered yourself? Both answers are the same size; neither is wrong.
  3. Write a line if you like; everyone closes their eyes and the day is put away.

Check-in · Weekly review

  1. Swipe left from home to get to Check-in. Pick your review day in Settings.
  2. On that day, in order: look at your week, pick what pulled you away, write a few lines to yourself, and file it under one thing.
  3. The next week starts by asking how that same thing went. Everything you write is kept under Topics, in The road section of your records.

Settings

  1. Tap an empty spot on home, then the gear at the top right.
  2. Practice: how long to sit, a countdown before starting, a buzz at the start, showing the time while sitting.
  3. Also reminders and sounds, whether home shows your level, your review day, and replaying the guide.

Lock screen and Dynamic Island

Still and Moving timers appear on the lock screen and in the Dynamic Island, and you can end them there.

  1. If you don't see them, open iPhone Settings, find the app and turn on Live Activities.
  2. Then go to Settings > Face ID & Passcode and make sure Live Activities is allowed when locked.
  3. Dynamic Island appears only on iPhones that have one. Other models show it on the lock screen.

Your data and other notes
