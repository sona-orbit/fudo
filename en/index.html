Fudo

Real strength is knowing you're here, moment to moment.

A few minutes a day of noticing that you're here. Sitting down, and while you walk, wash up or commute.

Why practice this way

  1. Focus that lasts.

    A little stillness, every day.

  2. Awake, and clear.

    Knowing plainly that you're here. You drift, you notice, you come back.

  3. Practice in quiet, and in your day.

    Sit for a few minutes. Then walk, wash up, ride the train, and still know you're here.

  4. When everyone is sprinting, settle and save your strength.

    When everyone runs out, you're still on your feet. Steadiness first, the rest after.

  5. Whatever road you're on, walk that one well.

    A divided mind is where the strain begins.

  6. Now and then, line yourself up again.

    Check in once a week, then turn what you found into a routine you can actually keep.

  7. No graduation. An apprentice every day.

    One day, when you are steady and strong, your quiet joy will be the world's delight.

Still: hold the flame and sit

Hold the flame for 0.8 seconds and you've started, with one light buzz. Close your eyes if you like. The screen goes dark except for a faint countdown.

When you notice you've drifted, tap anywhere. When you finish, a rose half-circle drops into today's tower, with a small bump on its edge for each drift.

Sit for 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, set your own from 1 to 120, or go untimed and hold the flame when you want to stop.

Moving: practice in daily life, right on your lock screen

Pick what you're doing: walking, washing dishes, commuting, eating, brushing teeth, tidying up, or type your own. Choose 5, 10, 15 or 30 minutes, or untimed, and tap Start.

You can close the app. The lock screen and Dynamic Island show the time, with buttons for Drifted and End.

Back in the app, tap Keep and a green block drops into today's tower, or tap Don't count to leave no record.

A line for today

365 short written lines, in nine languages. One is picked for you each day, on your phone. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Apple's built-in model helps choose, offline.

Save the ones you like. Decorate the card with items you've earned, put it on your lock screen, and tap Next card to flip to another.

Past lines stay with you, and you can view only the saved ones.

Did you remember yourself at some point today?

A tower a day, eyes closed at night

Each practice drops a stone: a rose half-circle for sitting, a green block for moving. The flame sits on top.

After sunset you can close the day: say whether you remembered yourself today, and add a line if you want. A blue bowl drops and everyone closes their eyes, top to bottom.

In your records, each month's towers stand in rows. Tap one to see every stone from that day.

It adds up: nine levels, far hills, weekly seals

Your sitting minutes and kept moving sessions add up to nine levels, from Spark to Fudo. Missing days never lowers your level or resets anything.

Every 300 minutes adds a stone to the home screen background, and over time they pile up into hills in the distance.

Each week, The road gives you three small things. Do all three and you get a weekly seal. At 4, 12, 26 and 52 seals you earn the four seasonal backgrounds: autumn maple, winter snow, spring cherry blossom, summer fireflies. Milestones and The shelf keep the date you earned each one.

Outfits for the flame and every stone

46 items and 3 characters, all earned by practice. None are for sale.

Glasses, hats and scarves go on the flame or on any stone. Props like the sword, the lantern and moss go anywhere on the screen; drag them and pinch to resize.

The editor is full screen; close the drawer to see the whole picture. Swap in a different flame: the four-eyed flame, the night flame or the twin flames.

Steady flame · 1,170 min

Sharing

Turn your dressed-up flame into a card that shows your level and your minutes.

Towers from your records become cards for a day, a week or a month. Cards show only dates and facts; your saved line and your notes stay off unless you add them.

They move

On the home screen the flame breathes and sways a little, and each stone bobs at its own pace. They blink, look around, doze off and glance at each other.

Tap one and it shakes, hops, or squashes and bounces back, and the others turn to look. If your iPhone is set to reduce motion, they stay still.

Weekly check-in and sound

Pick one day a week to check in: what pulled you away most this week, how long to sit next week, then confirm.

Background sounds: a low hum, brown noise, a hearth fire, a cave. Play them only while sitting, or switch sound off and keep just the haptics.

Try arranging

Who it's for, and who it isn't

A good fit if you

  • want a small daily moment to settle yourself
  • can't sit for long and want to start with 3 minutes
  • want to practice while walking, doing chores or commuting
  • like quiet things and don't want numbers and reminders chasing you

Not a fit if you

  • want a guided meditation course with voice instruction
  • need treatment or counseling. This is not a medical or mental health service
  • want leaderboards, challenges or streaks

Before you buy

More details: How to use the app · Support

Is there a subscription?

No. You pay once, and every feature and item is included. No in-app purchases.

Do I need internet or an account?

Neither. The app only goes online if you send us feedback.

How much time does it take?

As little as 3 minutes, or set your own from 1 minute. Moving practice fits into things you already do.

What if I skip a day?

Nothing happens. Your level and stones stay, and no reminder comes.

What language is the app in?

The screens are in Traditional Chinese for now. The daily lines follow your iPhone's language, in nine languages.
