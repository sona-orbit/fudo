Support

Stuck on something? Check the questions below. If your answer isn't here, write to us.

Using it for the first time, or looking for a button? See How to use the app.

Contact

In the app, swipe left from the home screen to the weekly check-in page, scroll down to About, and open the feedback form. Tell us what happened and tap Send. Leave a way to reach you if you'd like a reply.

Questions

The moving timer or line card doesn't show on my lock screen.

Open iPhone Settings, find the app, and turn on Live Activities. Then go to Settings > Face ID & Passcode and make sure Live Activities is allowed when locked.

Where is my data?

Your practice history, saved lines and outfits stay on this iPhone. Our servers don't have any of it. Deleting the app deletes it too.

How do I change the length?

Swipe left from the home screen to the weekly check-in page. Under the length setting, pick 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, or set your own from 1 to 120.

What does Untimed mean?

No timer. Hold the flame again whenever you want to stop.

What happens if I skip a few days?

Nothing. Your level, stones and items are all still there, and no reminder will come.

Not a medical service

Fudo is an everyday steadiness practice. It is not a medical or mental health service and can't diagnose or treat anything. If you're struggling or need help, please talk to a doctor or a qualified professional.
