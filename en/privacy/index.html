Privacy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

In short

Fudo needs no account and never sends your practice to a server.

What stays on your phone

Your sits and moving sessions, drift counts, the line you write at night, saved lines, outfits and settings all stay on this iPhone. Delete the app and they go with it.

The daily line

Each day's line is picked on your iPhone by Apple's built-in on-device model. Nothing goes online, and nothing passes through our servers.

What you send us

The app only goes online when you open the feedback form and tap Send. That sends your message, any contact you choose to leave, the app version, iOS version, device model, interface language, and a random ID made on your phone (used only to limit how often notes can be sent). It never includes your practice history or anything else. We use it only to reply and fix problems.

What we don't do

No ads, no tracking, no third-party analytics in the app. We don't sell or share your data.

This website

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits, which sets cookies. You can block it in your browser settings or with an extension. It has nothing to do with the app.

Contact

Questions? Write to us from the feedback form in the app.

Fudo is an iPhone app by Sona Orbit.
