# Fudo (不動修行) — a few steady minutes a day, a mindfulness practice app for iPhone

Fudo is an iPhone app for a daily steadiness practice. Hold the little flame and sit for a few minutes, noticing when you drift and coming back, or practice while you walk, wash the dishes or commute. It is for people who want a small, quiet daily moment without streaks, leaderboards or numbers chasing them. Pay once: no subscription, no ads, no account.

[Website (English)](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudo/en/) · [How to use](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudo/en/guide/) · [Support](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudo/en/support/) · [Privacy](https://sona-orbit.github.io/fudo/en/privacy/)

## Features

- **Still**: hold the flame for 0.8 seconds to start, tap anywhere when you notice you have drifted. Sit for 3, 5, 10 or 15 minutes, 1 to 120 minutes of your own, or untimed.
- **Moving**: practice while walking, washing dishes, commuting, eating, brushing teeth or tidying up, with the time and Drifted / End buttons on the Lock Screen and Dynamic Island.
- **Weekly check-in**: look at your week, write down what pulled you away, and your iPhone turns it into one line of your own, on the device.
- **Your line comes back**: after a sit with lots of drifting, your own line appears quietly on the home screen.
- **Four background sounds, haptics**, and day and night app icons.
- **No streaks** and no reminders unless you turn one on.
- **Private**: no account and no internet needed; your practice stays on your iPhone. Ten languages; iOS 17 or later.
- Not a guided meditation course, and not a medical or mental health service.
